Research

My research explores the relationship between people, digital technologies, and the systems through which technology is designed and governed. I am particularly interested in privacy, user-centred design, video games, artificial intelligence, and digital governance.

My work uses quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods approaches, including surveys, semi-structured interviews, statistical analysis, thematic analysis, scoping reviews, and evidence synthesis.

My doctoral research focuses specifically on privacy management and user control within video games, examining how players understand privacy and make decisions about their data in digital environments.

PhD Project

Informing User-Centred Design of Privacy Management Settings Within Video Games

Doctor of Philosophy, Coventry University
2022 – Present · Part-time · Self-funded

My PhD is a mixed-methods study examining privacy, governance, and user control in digital game environments. The research investigates how players understand and interact with privacy management settings and how these systems can better support users' expectations, behaviours, and decision-making.

The project incorporates survey research and semi-structured interviews alongside quantitative and qualitative analysis. The research has involved developing ethics documentation, recruiting participants, collecting and analysing data, and disseminating findings through academic publications and research events.
