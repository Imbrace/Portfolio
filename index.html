About Me

Hey, I am Jaimz.

I am a project support admin and academic researcher specialising in technology and immersive digital media. I have worked on numerous interdisciplinary research projects ranging from EU Horizon to industry collaborations, contributing to technical development, project administration, research design, and the creation of interactive and public-facing digital outputs.

My current researh interests include Human–Computer Interaction, digital privacy, video games, artificial intelligence, and digital governance. I am especially interested in the ways that emerging technologies influence people's behaviour and experiences, and how technology can be designed in more creative yet human-centred ways.

I am currently completing a part-time, self-funded PhD at Coventry University. My doctoral research explores privacy and user-centred design within video games.

Alongside my research, I have worked as a Teaching Fellow, supporting undergraduate students in areas including digital media, immersive technologies, and game development. I am also a Visiting Researcher at University College London working alongside Martin Dechant. More recently, I am a Research and Impact volunteer at Adenydd Charity, helping to evaluate past projects, document their impact, and communicate outcomes to support future funding and development.
