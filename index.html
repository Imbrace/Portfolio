About Me

I am Jaimz Winter, a researcher and PhD candidate at Coventry University. My academic background is in Game Technology, and my interests sit at the intersection of technology, people, and society.

I am particularly interested in understanding how people interact with digital technologies and how those technologies can be designed to better support people's needs, expectations, privacy, and wellbeing. I am a postgraduate researcher at Coventry University and academic researcher specialising in technology and immersive digital media. I have worked on numerous interdisciplinary research projects ranging from EU Horizon to industry collaborations, contributing to technical development, research design, and the creation of interactive and public-facing digital outputs. This website is a portfolio of some of the larger Unity projects developed for various research outputs.

My current interests include Human–Computer Interaction, digital privacy, video games, artificial intelligence, and digital governance. I am especially interested in the ways that emerging technologies influence people's behaviour and experiences, and how technology can be designed in more human-centred ways.

I am currently completing a part-time, self-funded PhD at Coventry University. My doctoral research explores privacy and user-centred design within video games.

Alongside my research, I have experience teaching undergraduate students in areas including digital media, immersive technologies, and game development. I enjoy working across disciplines and communicating ideas about technology to both specialist and non-specialist audiences.
